☕ Coffee Orders Data Analysis | Excel Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
This project explores coffee order sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, top customers, and country-level performance.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard.

🎯 Business Objectives
Track total sales over time.
Identify the top 5 customers by sales.
Compare sales performance across countries.
Support data-driven business decisions.

📊 Key Performance Analyses
1. Total Sales Over Time
Analyze how total sales evolve over time to identify trends, growth patterns, and potential seasonal variations.

2. Top 5 Customers
Identify the five highest-value customers based on sales to understand customer contribution to overall revenue.

3. Sales by Country
Compare sales across countries to identify the strongest-performing markets and potential growth opportunities.

🛠️ Tools & Techniques
Microsoft Excel
Data cleaning and preparation
PivotTables and PivotCharts
Data visualization
Interactive dashboard filters
